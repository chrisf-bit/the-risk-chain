How it works

What we build, the three ways to run it, the benefits, and the few things we need from you to make it work.

The three ways to run it

Same training and reporting either way. You pick the delivery that fits your setup.

Hosted by us
Recommended
We run everything. Your staff open the training from a link we provide, and the results flow straight to your dashboard. Nothing for your IT team to build or maintain.
Your staff sign in to our platform. Simplest and most reliable, and it "just works".
Through your own training system
We plug into your LMS. The training runs inside the system your staff already use. Modern systems can also pass the richer results through to your dashboard; older ones will still record who completed it and their score.
Your staff sign in to your existing system. We check its compatibility with you up front.
Reporting only
You deliver, we make sense of the data. We take the results and turn them into the dashboard and coaching reports.
No sign-in built by us. Best where systems are already in place.

What we are building

The simulations

Realistic, decision-based training scenarios your staff play through, with a supportive coaching debrief at the end.

The manager dashboard

Team readiness at a glance, who needs support, the shared strengths and gaps, and recommended next courses.

A secure delivery layer

A light, secure way for staff to reach the training and for results to reach your dashboard. Works on any device, even when the wifi drops.

Best-in-class data protection

UK data handling built in, with the option to run it without ever storing your staff's names.

What we are not building, and you do not need: a whole new learning management system for you to run. This sits alongside what you already have. If you have no system, hosted-by-us covers everything.

The benefits

  • Low burden on your team. With hosted-by-us there is nothing to install or maintain.
  • Reliable results. Training saves on the device and sends when it is back online, so nothing is lost on a weak connection.
  • Secure and compliant. Data protection and access controls built in, ready for your DPO and for inspection.
  • Actionable insight. Not just "who finished", but who is ready, who needs help, and exactly with what.
  • Fits your world. Run it our way or through your own systems, on the devices your staff already use.

What we need from you to make it work

If we host it (recommended)

  • A list of who needs the training (or just share the link with your teams). That is essentially it.

If you run it through your own system

  • A quick check with us that your system is compatible (we will confirm what it can and cannot pass through).
  • Your IT team to approve us as a trusted supplier. We give them a simple sheet listing exactly what to allow, a quick tick-box, not a project.

For everyone

  • A signed data agreement (standard), and a decision on whether reporting uses names or stays anonymous.
  • A named contact on your side to get set up and answer any access questions.
