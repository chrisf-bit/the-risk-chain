PRPracticE Ready

We don't just train your staff. We show you they're actually ready.

Most care training is click-through-and-forget: people watch a video, tick a box, and you cross your fingers they would do the right thing on shift. PracticE Ready is different. We put your staff into the real situations that go wrong, like a resident starting to choke at teatime, and let them make the actual decisions, safely, on screen. They get honest, supportive coaching on how they did. And you get a clear picture of who is genuinely ready and who needs a hand.

How it comes together

1

The training your staff do

A realistic, first-person simulation. They step into a shift, read the records, make the calls, and live with the outcome, then get a plain, encouraging debrief: what they did well, and the one thing to work on. It feels real, so the learning sticks.

2

The insight you get optional add-on

A simple dashboard for managers. At a glance: how ready each team is, who needs support, and the exact skills your people are strongest and weakest at, so your training spend goes where it is actually needed. It even points each person to the next course that would help them most.

Why it matters

  • Real readiness, not a tick box. You learn whether people can actually do it, not just that they finished.
  • Learning that sticks. Practising a real decision beats watching a video.
  • Spend training where it counts. See the shared gaps and target them, instead of training everyone in everything.
  • Evidence for inspection. Clear, exportable proof of competence across your workforce.
  • Supportive, not punishing. Staff are coached and encouraged, never ranked to shame them.
  • Fits how you work. Run it through us or through your own systems, on any device.
"Other training tells you people finished it. We tell you whether they can actually do it, and what to do about the ones who cannot yet."

Think of it like learning to drive. It is the difference between a written test and getting in the car. We let your staff make the mistakes in the simulator, so they do not make them with a real person.
